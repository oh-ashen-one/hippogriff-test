Hippogriff — every version Model: the Tripo-generated hippogriff GLB — 50,744 triangles, 1 material, 1 texture, no rig; scaled to a ~2.5 m creature. Brief: 120 frames at 24 fps, one continuous flight shot with gravity readable in every frame. Two contenders built it independently — Blender 5.2 LTS and Houdini 22.0.451.

BLENDER Full flight — final render

The delivered shot. Hand-built 30-bone rig, procedural skinning, FK-baked asymmetric wingbeat, cloth tail streamer, procedural sky and cloud decks. Rendered, not a viewport capture.

HOUDINI Full flight — deform rig + vellum sim

Procedural VEX deform rig, phase-warped wingbeat, simulated vellum tail, Solaris stage. The Apprentice licence blocks Karma lighting and every geometry exporter, so the geometry was extracted directly and shaded in Blender. The motion is Houdini's.

RECOLOURED Animated GLB — hippogriff palette

The model's own texture is essentially monochrome charcoal (mean RGB 0.159 / 0.146 / 0.171, max 0.43), so it was recoloured: golden beak and talons, brown feathered head and wings, chestnut horse hindquarters with dapple, black hooves. glTF 2.0, 1 mesh, 3 animations, 30-joint skin, 4096 texture embedded.
